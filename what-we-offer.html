Wellbeing services

What else do we offer?

Explore the other ways Clever Fox Therapies may be able to support you.

Hypnotherapy

Hypnotherapy is available as a standalone therapy or, where appropriate, alongside counselling. Hypnotherapy skills can also help with relaxation, sleep and letting go of negative thoughts.

Hypnotherapy is not about losing control. It is a deeply relaxed state in which you remain aware and can choose whether to take part. You can bring yourself out of hypnosis at any time. Some people use this calm state to explore and change thoughts, beliefs, emotions or behaviours that affect everyday life.

Reiki

Reiki is a gentle, holistic practice that aims to support relaxation and wellbeing. It is non-invasive and can offer a quiet space to pause, unwind and reconnect with yourself.

Holistic massage

Holistic massage is a nurturing treatment that considers the whole person. Gentle, flowing techniques can promote relaxation and offer time to reconnect with yourself in a calm setting.
