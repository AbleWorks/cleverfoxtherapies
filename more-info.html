How am I qualified to work with you?

Whether you choose me or someone else, it's important to check that your counsellor is qualified. Counsellors should belong to a professional body with a code of practice and have completed recognised training.

Knowing about my background is important. I am an Integrative Therapeutic Counsellor and continue to update my training, with a focus on anxiety, trauma and ADHD. I teach the diploma in counselling, and I am an accredited member of the National Counselling and Psychotherapy Society (NCPS) and a member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP).

I qualified as a nurse before returning to university to study biology and then completing postgraduate teacher training. I also have a Diploma in Clinical Hypnosis, training in Thought Field Therapy and Past Life Regression Therapy, NHS Personalised Care Institute-accredited wellbeing coach training, and qualifications as a meditation teacher and Reiki Master Teacher.

Coaching or counselling?

Cognitive coaching uses many of the skills and techniques used in counselling. One of the main differences is that coaching is forward-looking and solution-focused, with the aim of helping change happen.

Sometimes we need to understand patterns, beliefs or past experiences that may be holding you back, and explore ways to change negative thought patterns. Coaching focuses on self-awareness and practical action to help close the gap between your potential and where you are now.

There is overlap between counselling and coaching. Coaching may be more appropriate when you feel stuck and want support to move forward, rather than to address issues affecting your mental wellbeing.

Working with young people

Having worked in education for many years, I care deeply about the mental health and wellbeing of young people.

Common reasons for seeking support include anxiety and overwhelm, relationship or friendship difficulties, and feeling unable to cope with school pressures.

Working together, we can explore where thoughts come from, understand feelings and develop ways to manage negative thoughts—skills that can be useful throughout life.
