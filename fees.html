Counselling: what will it cost and how long will I need?

I try to keep my pricing simple. I offer a shorter session for young people who may feel overwhelmed by a longer session, and this can be adjusted to their needs.

Appointments are available face to face, via Zoom or by telephone. The initial 30-minute discovery call is free, so we can see whether I am the right person for you and whether I feel I can support what you need.

It's impossible to say in advance how many sessions you will need. Some people have one or two and feel ready to make changes; others benefit from more sessions. We discuss this together, but the choice is always yours.
