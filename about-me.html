A little about me

About Me

A supportive space to find your way forward

Hi, thanks for visiting Clever Fox. For over twenty years I worked in education as a teacher and wellbeing practitioner. During that time I supported young people and other professionals who found education challenging and often exhausting.

I believe people can find good mental health and take control of their future with the right support. I am passionate about helping people overcome life's obstacles, recognise their strengths and find their way forward to lasting change.

I have a particular interest in trauma and anxiety, and training in ADHD and anxiety. I work with many clients diagnosed with ADHD later in life.

I offer a supportive, non-judgmental space to talk about how things are for you. Together we can explore your feelings and barriers, and develop long-term strategies for positive change.
