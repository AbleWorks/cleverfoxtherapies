How can I help?

Hello and welcome.

It's a big step recognising you could do with support. Just by having a look, you've made a step forward. Whether you choose to come to me or someone else, you deserve a big well done for looking for the person to help you make the changes you want and need.

Below you can see what I can help with. For a bit more on how, hop over to the More Info page.

Are you feeling…

  • Anxious?
  • Overwhelmed?
  • Pulled in all directions and unable to say no?

The most important starting point is recognising that you need support and want to make changes.

I am a trained integrative counsellor, accredited by the National Counselling and Psychotherapy Society and a member of the BACP. I have a holistic approach, using a variety of counselling methods and biological knowledge to make sense of how you feel. I will support you to take control of your life and create a strong, confident you. I have a particular interest in ADHD and anxiety; more about my qualifications can be found on the More Info page.

If you are looking for help with a young person, give me a call or drop me a message to talk it over. With over twenty years in education, I have the experience and passion to help them find their way forward and develop long-term strategies they can use throughout life.

Sometimes busy lives, family or a preference to work from home without travel can make getting to appointments difficult. I work face to face, via Zoom or on the telephone, so I can support you wherever you are.
