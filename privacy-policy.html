Privacy information

Information you choose to provide

If you contact the practice by email or telephone, your contact details and the information you choose to share may be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss possible appointments. The website enquiry form opens your own email application; it does not transmit or store form entries on this website.

Please do not include sensitive health information in an initial website enquiry. Information shared later as part of counselling or another service may be handled under separate client records and confidentiality arrangements.
